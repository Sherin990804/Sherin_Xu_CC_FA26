Spring

A creative coding animation featuring rain, grass, and dandelions drifting in the wind.

Grass & Wind: Combined sin() and bezier() to simulate grass and flower stems swaying naturally in the wind.

Dandelion Animation: Created a Dandelion class and used arrays to manage individual seeds. Each seed changes from an attached state to a flying state, with independent velocity and rotation.

Animation Loop: Used frameCount and conditional statements to control seed release and automatically restart the animation.
