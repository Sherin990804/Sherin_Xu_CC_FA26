For week2_1.js, I designed a triangular wireframe that rotates around the center of the canvas, as well as a circle that can be drawn by dragging the mouse.

For week2_2.js, I designed lines that radiate outward from the center of the canvas; clicking anywhere on the canvas generates a line at that position with a random color, width, and opacity.

For week2_3.js, I designed a circular pattern that moves within the canvas boundaries, with the x-coordinate generated randomly and the y-coordinate cycling up and down. The diameter of each circle is randomly generated between 10 and 255, and the color of each circle changes dynamically based on its position.

For week2_4.js, I designed a circular pattern that moves within the canvas boundaries, with the x-coordinate cycling left and right and the y-coordinate cycling up and down. The diameter of each circle varies within a range of 16, and the color of each circle changes dynamically based on its position.

Press any key on the keyboard to reset the screen.
