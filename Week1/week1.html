This is a website of artist Zach Lieberman, whose work explore sthe intersection of human movement, multisensory perception, and dynamics.

Three links to his creative code projects Eyewriter , Play the world and Hag-Seed.

What I find interesting about Zach's artist work is that his code is much more than just tech support behind the scenes. It acts as a medium that stretches our senses (whether through body movements or sounds) , lets us re-experience the world and help us live better.

I previously created an interactive project using Arduino and TouchDesigner that turned emotions into visual patterns. It helped me visually record and face my own feelings, making it a real process of self-discovery and perceiving the world around me.

I love how technology can connect so deeply with human senses and perception.

To be honest, coming from a different major, I haven't had much exposure to great art pieces, and I still feel a bit insecure about how I understand or interpret creative project. This is something I really hope to work on in this class. I'm looking forward to learning about the professor's favorite works and exploring more creative projects together!
